“Account Hub” — App concept directions

Four interactive directions for making “Account” feel like You at MECCA — plus today’s live experience for comparison. Rendered in a true iPhone 15 Pro frame (393×852pt with Dynamic Island), skinned with the real MECCA tokens, with a proper line-icon set. Carousels swipe, accordions expand, tabs and chips respond — tap around.

Option A · smallest change, highest leverage

Account Hub overlay

A value layer sits above the existing settings: an identity header, value tiles, and a Profile Fit entry — settings stay below, untouched. The PDP gains a “why this matches you” module that closes the confidence loop.

What it optimises for

Low-risk activation of value members already have + shopping confidence.

IA risk

Minimal — ships as an overlay; settings list is unchanged below.

Watch-out

Hub + Settings can feel stacked unless visually integrated.

Option B · stronger membership posture

Loyalty-first hub

Beauty Loop becomes the spine: a persistent points wallet, a dominant tier header, and rewards up front. The profile is framed as the way to improve your matches. Home carries the same wallet so value is always one glance away. B3 benchmarks the exact IKEA Family home pattern — value tiles, activities, projects — reskinned in MECCA’s system as a layout reference.

What it optimises for

Retention and value exchange — making membership feel like the identity.

IA risk

Moderate — wallet pattern must be consistent across Home + hub.

Watch-out

Can over-weight loyalty vs shopping confidence; align with Home widget.

Option D · newcomer header + activity wallet

Loyalty home — newcomer header

Option D keeps the B3 loyalty-home layout but swaps the purple hero for B1’s Beauty Newcomer status pattern (tier + points + progress). The Wallet moves down into the activities grid — sitting under Purchases — and a new Upcoming bookings activity is added with a calendar icon.

What it optimises for

A calmer, lighter entry that still leads with tier status and points.

IA risk

Low–moderate — reuses proven B1 + B3 patterns; wallet demoted to an activity.

Watch-out

Wallet is less prominent than in B3 — confirm that matches its usage.

Why this falls short
  • No identity or membership. Your tier, points and rewards are invisible — nothing says “you” at MECCA.
  • Value is buried. Beauty Loop status, points balance and vouchers aren’t surfaced, so there’s no reason to return.
  • Flat, undifferentiated list. Every row carries equal weight; nothing guides attention to what matters most.
  • No personalisation. Nothing reflects what you’ve bought or your beauty profile — no matches or recommendations.
  • Feels like admin, not a destination. Settings-first framing makes the screen purely transactional.
